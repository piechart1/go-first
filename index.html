How a day goes

Each challenge is something real to do with other people, pitched at your level. Each one has a smaller version too, for days when a smaller step suits you better.

  1. Pick one

    Three challenges each morning, from three different areas. Choose the one that fits what your day already holds.

  2. Guess, then go

    Before you start, guess how hard it will be. Add a when and where if that helps, then do it whenever it fits.

    How hard do you think this will be? 6 / 10
    EasyVery hard

  3. See how it went

    Afterwards, rate how hard it actually was. Over time you can compare what you expected with what happened.

    Example You expected 6. It was 3. If it didn’t happen, record that too. It still counts as turning up.

Five areas, each at your level

A few starting questions set a level for each area. Levels move up as challenges get easier for you, and down if they’re too much. You can switch any area off.

  • ConnectStarting conversations with people you don’t know.
  • ExpressBeing seen, and saying what you think.
  • AskMaking requests, and saying no.
  • DeepenOpening up with people already in your life.
  • LeadSuggesting plans and taking the initiative.

Made to be kind about off days

  • No streak to loseNo points and no leaderboard. A day you record honestly counts, whatever happened.
  • Keep what worksAdd a challenge you’d like to keep doing to your stack, a short list of habits you record with one tap.
  • Private by designEverything you record stays on your phone. No account, no analytics and no advertising.
  • FreeGo First is free, with no in-app purchases.
