What Go First is

Go First suggests small social challenges and lets you record how they went. It is designed to help build confidence and social skills.

It isn’t medical or psychological advice, and it isn’t a treatment for anxiety or any other condition. It doesn’t replace a doctor, psychologist or other health professional. If things feel hard to manage, please speak to one.

If you are in immediate danger or thinking about harming yourself, call your local emergency number. In Australia that is 000, and Lifeline is available on 13 11 14.

Using the challenges

Every challenge is a suggestion. You decide which to take on, and you are responsible for what you do.

  • Use your own judgement about what is safe and appropriate where you are.
  • Treat other people with respect. If someone seems busy, uncomfortable or not interested, leave it there.
  • Don’t do anything that is unlawful, or that puts you or anyone else at risk.
  • If a challenge doesn’t suit you, choose another, or mark it “Not for me” so it isn’t offered again.
